01 — ORIENTATION

What is metal forming formability?

Formability is the ability of a material to undergo the deformation demanded by a manufacturing process while remaining free of unacceptable defects.

operational definition
F = { process parameters | flow is stable and the part meets its specification }

It is a process window, not one number from a tensile test. The same sheet can be safe in a drawing operation and fail in a stretching operation.

stable plastic flowlocalization → fracture
01

Can it reach the required strains without diffuse necking, localized necking, wrinkling, or fracture?

Which combinations of die radius, lubrication, speed, temperature, and force produce an acceptable part?

Why a good tensile elongation can still fail

A 1.20 mm sheet has uniform elongation of 22%, but the cup wall develops a crack near the punch radius. The tensile number describes one uniaxial path; the tool imposes changing combinations of drawing, bending, and biaxial stretching.

blank diameterD₀ = 90 mm
punch diameterdₚ = 50 mm
drawing ratioβ = D₀ / dₚ = 1.80
interpretationnear the process limit
Engineering decision

Reduce β with a larger punch or redraw step, improve lubrication, or change the blank-holder force. Then validate with a cup test and local strain measurement.

The useful question is: what deformation path does the process impose, and what mechanism ends stable plastic flow?
